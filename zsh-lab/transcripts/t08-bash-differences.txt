t08 — Bash habits under zsh: same text, different shell

### arrays are numbered from...
--- bash:
a0=red a1=green count=3
--- zsh:
a0= a1=red count=3

### an unquoted variable holding two words, used as arguments
--- bash:
 1 notes.md
 1 summary.txt
 2 total
--- zsh:
wc: 'notes.md summary.txt': No such file or directory

### a glob that matches nothing
--- bash:
*.log
--- zsh:
zsh:1: no matches found: *.log

### echo and backslash escapes
--- bash:
one\ttwo
--- zsh:
one	two

### the result of the last command, in a test
--- bash:
failed-as-expected
--- zsh:
failed-as-expected

### string equality with a pattern on the right
--- bash:
matches
--- zsh:
matches

### the escape hatch: ask for Bash's rules explicitly
$ zsh -c 'setopt sh_word_split ksh_arrays; a=(red green blue); echo "a0=${a[0]}"; files="notes.md summary.txt"; wc -l $files'
a0=red
 1 notes.md
 1 summary.txt
 2 total
